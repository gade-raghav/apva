# APVA Governance

## Roles

- **Users** — anyone running APVA. Feedback via issues is welcome.
- **Contributors** — anyone who submits a pull request, issue, doc or review.
- **Maintainers** — listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Maintainers review and
  merge changes, cut releases and set the roadmap.

The project was founded by Raghav Gade, who is the lead maintainer.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor can be nominated by any maintainer after sustained, high-quality
contributions (typically several merged non-trivial PRs and reviews over ~3 months).
Nomination is approved by a majority of existing maintainers.

## Decision making

- Day-to-day changes: lazy consensus — a PR with one maintainer approval and no
  unresolved objections for 2 working days may be merged.
- Significant changes (new APIs, CRD changes, governance changes): a design proposal
  in `docs/proposals/`, open for at least 1 week, approved by a majority of maintainers.
- If consensus cannot be reached, the lead maintainer makes the final call.

## Removal

Maintainers inactive for 6 months may be moved to emeritus status by majority vote.

## Code of Conduct

All participants follow the [CNCF Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Changes

This document may evolve as the project grows (for example on joining the CNCF
Sandbox), with approval from a majority of maintainers.
